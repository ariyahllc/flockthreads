# Faith-Apparel T-Shirt Shop — Launch Brief

Reference competitor: bluvap.com (Shopify, faith/Christian tees, $22.99 base price, bundle discounts, ~2-4 week shipping suggesting overseas bulk manufacturing, no B2B/group-order flow).

## Differentiator
Church/ministry group ordering with logo customization, on a cleaner interface than existing group-order tools (Fourthwall, Spiritwear.com, Greek Gear, Printify Pop-Up Store). Plus non-faith occasion lines (Mother's/Father's Day etc.) in the same tasteful, subtle-humor visual style.

## Fulfillment model: two tracks
1. **DTC catalog** (individual buyers, faith-themed tees) — bulk/private-label overseas production for margin, once designs are validated via POD testing. Cotton/poly blend TBD by owner.
2. **Church/group orders** (event-driven, hard deadlines: VBS, Easter, mission trips, youth camp) — needs domestic, fast-turnaround printing (regional screen printer or Printful/Gelato US). Overseas 2-4 week lead times will blow event deadlines.

## POD vs. bulk sourcing

| | Print-on-demand | Bulk / private label |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront cash | ~$0, no inventory | $500–5,000+ per run (MOQ dependent) |
| Margin | ~40-50% | ~60-70%+ once sold through |
| Quality control | Printify varies by print partner; Printful more consistent (own facilities); Gelato consistent within its network, fastest regional shipping (140+ facilities) | Full control over fabric weight, blend, print method |
| Shipping | Domestic POD: 3-7 days. Overseas bulk: 2-4 weeks | Same, depends on manufacturing location |
| Best for | Testing designs, low-risk launch, long-tail catalog | Proven bestsellers, time-sensitive church/event orders |

Recommendation: hybrid. POD (Printful or Gelato, not Printify) for testing new designs; bulk order proven bestsellers once validated; domestic fast-turnaround printing for church group orders specifically.

## Church/ministry channel — build plan
- Separate "For Churches" storefront section, not just a generic wholesale page.
- Live customizer app: Inkybay or Customily (top-rated Shopify apps for logo upload + real-time preview).
- Tiered bulk pricing shown automatically as order quantity grows.
- Shareable order-collection link so a volunteer doesn't have to gather payments manually.
- Optional fundraising kickback (% of order value back to the church/ministry) — a proven driver in this niche, and something Bluvap-style DTC brands don't offer.

## Other differentiation ideas
- Personalized "verse of the day" / birthday-verse tees.
- Ministry/worship-team pre-bundled packs (sizes/colors kitted like existing bundle mechanic, but B2B-flavored).
- Spanish-language faith apparel line — underserved relative to English-only competitors.
- Modest give-back model (% of profit to a rotating ministry/charity).

## Running it on autopilot
- POD/bulk partner handles physical fulfillment — no inventory touch.
- Klaviyo or Shopify Email: pre-built flows (welcome, abandoned cart) + seasonal campaign flows that fire automatically.
- Content calendar with designs queued 4-6 weeks ahead of each occasion (Mother's Day, Father's Day, Easter, Christmas, back-to-school, graduation) to absorb production/shipping lead time.
- AI tools (Midjourney/Ideogram) or a retained designer to keep new designs flowing without manual design work each cycle.
- Optional: scheduled reminder ~1 month before each occasion to greenlight that season's designs.

## Non-faith occasion lines — tone guidance
Small-scale typography or a single clever line (e.g., left-chest print) rather than big loud graphics. Dry, self-aware humor over slapstick. Keep faith and non-faith lines on the same blanks, print style, and photography so the brand reads as one cohesive shop despite multiple message categories.

## Open items to decide next
- Cotton/poly blend spec for the DTC line.
- Manufacturer shortlist (Alibaba/1688 sourcing vs. POD platform) with sample/QC checkpoints (GSM weight, wash-test shrinkage, print durability).
- Domestic printer for church/group orders.
- Store cost model: Shopify + apps (~$39-150+/mo), Inkybay/Customily customizer app fee, Klaviyo tier, ad budget ($500-2,000+/mo to start).
