The shipping treatment is the whole ballgame. Both rows are real; pick your policy deliberately.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Flag:** POD single unit + free shipping + any paid-ad CAC ≈ break-even or loss. A $24.99 free-ship POD order nets **$8.55** before ad cost; one $9–12 Meta click-to-buy wipes it out. Two defenses below: bundles and the bulk path.

### 4. Three pricing scenarios

**Scenario 1 — Base (single DTC tee).** $24.99, free ship only over $50 (i.e., single tee ships paid). Contribution $13.05 (POD) / $15.97 (bulk). This is your ad landing-page hero but your *worst* economics — don't build the P&L on it.

**Scenario 2 — Bundle (drives AOV over the free-ship line).**

| Offer | Price | COGS | Pay fee | Ship (1 parcel) | **Contribution** | CM% | $/shirt contrib |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 for $45 (POD) | $45.00 | $21.84 | $1.61 | $6.60 | **$14.95** | 33% | $7.48 |
| 3 for $63 (bulk-sourced) | $63.00 | $24.00 | $2.13 | $8.70 | **$28.17** | 45% | $9.39 |

Bundle lifts AOV from $24.99 → $45–63 and contribution +75–120% vs a single free-ship order, while one parcel amortizes shipping. App: **Fast Bundle** or **Bundler** (~$15/mo). Make the 2-pack the default "gift + keep one" for Mother's/Father's Day.

**Scenario 3 — Church/group bulk (screen print, tiered).** Prepaid via Shopify draft order, near-zero CAC, no returns — so lower GM% is fine. Fees ~2.9% (~$0.40–0.60/shirt) not shown separately.

| Qty tier | Price/shirt | COGS/shirt *(assumption)* | GP/shirt | GM% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12–24 | $19.99 | $12.50 | $7.49 | 37% |
| 25–49 | $17.99 | $11.50 | $6.49 | 36% |
| 50–99 | $15.99 | $10.75 | $5.24 | 33% |
| 100–249 | $13.99 | $9.75 | $4.24 | 30% |
| 250+ | $12.49 | $8.75 | $3.74 | 30% |

**Fundraising kickback — do NOT absorb it.** A 10% kickback on the $15.99 tier is $1.60, which eats a third of your $5.24 margin. Instead structure it **additively**: the church sells at *tier price + kickback* and keeps the difference.

| Tier | Your take (tier price) | Church adds | Congregant pays | Church keeps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50–99 | $15.99 | $4.00 | $19.99 | $4.00 |
| 100–249 | $13.99 | $4.00 | $17.99 | $4.00 |

Your margin is untouched; the church gets a clean, visible cut. This is the standard fundraiser model and it's cleaner for their bookkeeping too. If a group insists on baked-in %, cap it at 10% and only on the 50+ tiers.

So **~25–35 tees/month covers your fixed software + design**. That's a low bar — the real constraint is customer-acquisition cost, which sits *on top*:

- **CAC guardrail:** keep blended CAC under **$10** or single-unit POD orders lose money. Target CAC : contribution ≥ **1 : 2** — bundles (CM $15–28) give the ad headroom singles don't.
- **Per-design kill-rule (POD test):** a design must recoup its ~$75 art cost = **6 units**; kill/retire any design under **10 units in its first 4 weeks** before it clogs the catalog.
- **Bulk-buy trigger:** private label saves ~$2.92/unit ($10.92 → $8.00). A 300-unit MOQ ≈ **$1,650 cash** upfront. Only commit when a design proves **≥100 units/quarter**, and order ~one quarter of demand. Below that velocity, POD's zero-inventory beats the tied-up cash.
