## 1. Concept & Angle

**The insight:** Mother's Day tee buying splits into two bad options — sentimental cursive over-sharing ("World's Best Mom" in script) or ironic mom-humor that trivializes the day. Neither works for a woman who takes her faith seriously and doesn't want a billboard on her chest. The faith market specifically is dominated by loud graphics that read as costume, not clothing.

**Our angle:** *Motherhood as quiet calling, not performance.* Left-chest typographic pieces a mom would wear on a Tuesday, not just May 9. The scripture is real and load-bearing — chosen for what it says about the actual weight of mothering (tired, faithful, showing up), not decorative verse-as-wallpaper. Humor, where present, is dry and self-aware and never at the expense of the sincerity.

**The buying reality (drives everything below):**

| Fact | Design consequence |
|---|---|
| ~70%+ of Mother's Day apparel is bought as a gift, not self-purchase | Buyer doesn't know her size. Push the gift-card safety net + generous exchange copy. Unisex sizing is a liability; go fitted-women's primary. |
| Gift-buyer is often the husband or adult child, shopping late, low confidence | Product page must answer "will she actually wear this?" in one image. Lead with on-body, not flat-lay. |
| She sees it on the shelf/box before she wears it | Fold + hang presentation matters. Left-chest print reads well folded. |
| Church moms often buy in small clusters (women's ministry, MOPS groups) | One design must work as a 6-12 pc soft group order. That's the bridge to the church channel. |

**Anti-goals — kill on sight:**
- Cursive script fonts, watercolor florals, distressed/grunge texture
- "Mama" in a trendy retro font (saturated to death — Etsy has tens of thousands)
- Verse references without the verse doing work
- Anything that reads as a costume rather than a garment
- Humor that implies mothering is a burden she's enduring

**Cohesion check:** Every piece must sit beside *Fearfully Made* (Psalm 139:14, left-chest) on the same rack and read as one brand. Same type discipline, same restraint, same print scale.

## 4. Asset Spec

**The non-negotiable:** These are typographic pieces. **Do not let an image generator produce the final type.** AI garbles letterforms — a misrendered "amén" or a broken Proverbs reference is a brand-killing defect on a sincerity-driven line. AI is for *mood and layout exploration only*. Final type is set in Illustrator/Affinity Designer from a licensed font, converted to outlines.

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| **Print file format** | PNG, transparent background |
| **Resolution** | 300 DPI at final print size |
| **Left-chest canvas** | 1200 × 1200 px @ 300 DPI (4" × 4" safe area); artwork occupies 2.75–3.25" wide |
| **Full-front canvas (if needed)** | 4500 × 5400 px @ 300 DPI (15" × 18") |
| **Color mode** | sRGB (Printful/Gelato DTG requires RGB, not CMYK) |
| **Ink colors** | Deep Charcoal `#2B2B2B` (not pure black — reads softer, more expensive). Bone `#EDE7DC`. |
| **Type treatment** | Convert to outlines. No live text in print file. |
| **Minimum stroke weight** | 1.5 pt at final size — thinner will break up on DTG and heather blends |
| **Fonts** | Serif with a modern editorial feel. Candidates: **Untitled Serif**, **Freight Text Pro**, **Tiempos Text**, **GT Sectra**. Verify commercial/merch license before purchase — many foundries require an extended license for apparel. Budget $50–$300. |
| **Spanish typography** | Font must have proper `é` and `Á` glyph coverage. Test-set `amén` and `fuerza y dignidad` before committing to any font purchase. |
| **Neck label** | Inside back neck, brand mark + verse ref, 2" wide, Printful "inside label" add-on (~$2.49/unit) |
| **Naming** | `MD27_[##]_[name]_[color]_[placement].png` → e.g. `MD27_01_called_heatherdust_leftchest.png` |

**Mockups needed per design:** 1 on-body hero (lifestyle, natural light), 1 flat-lay folded, 1 close-up print detail, 1 model-back (if neck label). Use Printful's mockup generator for the baseline; supplement the hero shot with a real photo of the bulk-run sample if possible.

## 3. The Shipping Decision (do this first — it sets the price)

You cannot charge $24.99 + $5.95 shipping to a husband buying one shirt on May 2 and expect him to convert. Shipping-cost shock at checkout is the single biggest killer on a gift-buyer, low-confidence, one-unit cart.

| Approach | Effective price to buyer | Contribution/unit | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| $24.99 + $4.95 shipping charged | $29.94 | $7.05 | Highest margin, worst conversion on a 1-unit gift cart |
| $24.99, free ship over $50 | varies | mixed | Forces a 2-unit cart the gift buyer doesn't want |
| **$26.99, free shipping always** | **$26.99** | **$7.83** | **Recommended.** Cleaner story, better contribution than charging $4.95 on a $24.99 tee, no checkout shock |

**Recommendation: raise core from $24.99 → $26.99 and make shipping free sitewide on this capsule.** The brief inherits $24.99 from the pricing hand-down, but $24.99 was set without the shipping line. $26.99 free-ship nets you more per unit *and* converts better than $24.99 + shipping. It also sits above the Etsy $19.99–$24.99 sea and reinforces "clothing, not merch."

If you're unwilling to move off $24.99, keep it — but then contribution drops to $6.14/unit and break-even volume rises ~28%. See §6.

| **Gross margin (COGS + ship)** | **$10.35** | **38.3%** |

**Read this line carefully.** At a blended $8 CAC on a single-unit order, this capsule makes **nothing**. That is not a modeling error — it is the actual economics of a $27 POD tee sold to a cold gift-buyer on paid social. A $27 AOV cannot carry paid acquisition.

**The model only works on one of three legs:**

| Leg | What it does | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Organic/email/owned** | CAC ≈ $0–2 | ≥60% of capsule units (A) |
| **Bundle (AOV lift)** | 2 units amortizes shipping + CAC across two shirts | ≥25% of orders |
| **Bulk/group orders** | 12–36 pc at once; CAC per unit collapses | ≥15% of units |

**Do not run cold paid acquisition on single-unit tees at $26.99.** Paid social on this capsule should point at the bundle or the church channel, not the core SKU.
