# Our 1%

One percent of every order we take goes to Christian relief and development work. Not one percent of profit, and not only on church orders. One percent of the order, every order.

You choose where it goes. The option sits in your cart, just above checkout, and takes one click. If you would rather not choose, we send it to Compassion International.

## It costs you nothing

The 1% comes out of our margin, not your total. Nothing is added at checkout, and we are not collecting a donation from you or on any charity's behalf. You buy a shirt; we give a percentage of what you paid.

## Where it can go

Every organisation below holds a Charity Navigator 4-star rating, ECFA accreditation, or both.

- **Compassion International** — one-to-one child sponsorship through local churches.
- **Samaritan's Purse** — emergency relief after disasters, war, and famine.
- **World Vision** — clean water, nutrition, and child wellbeing.
- **Convoy of Hope** — hunger relief and disaster response.
- **The Salvation Army** — local community aid, shelter, and food.

## How we report it

We total what is owed each quarter and send it in one payment per organisation, then publish the amounts on this page. If a quarter's total for any one charity comes to less than $25, we roll it into the next quarter so more of it survives the transfer.

FlockThreads is not affiliated with these organisations, and naming them here is not an endorsement by them of us.
