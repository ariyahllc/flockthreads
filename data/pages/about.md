# Our Story

FlockThreads started with a bad t-shirt.

A children's ministry leader we know spent hours emailing a design back and forth with a local shop, made three phone calls to place one group order, waited two weeks, and got shirts whose prints cracked after a few washes. The design was fine. The shirt let it down.

We think faith apparel should be the opposite of that: quietly well-made, easy to order, and good enough that you actually want to wear it again.

## What we believe

**Quality is the whole point.** We print on soft, side-seamed Bella+Canvas blanks and stand behind every print. If it's not right, we reprint it free.

**Tasteful, not loud.** Small left-chest type. A little dry humor. Designs that read as clothing first, message second.

**Made for groups, not just individuals.** Churches, ministries, and small groups shouldn't have to fight a clunky ordering process. We're building the easiest way to outfit your people.

## The give-back

A portion of every order goes back to ministry. Your shirt does a little good before it ever reaches your closet.
