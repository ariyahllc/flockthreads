## Shipping

Every tee is made to order and printed just for you. Orders typically **print and ship within 2–5 business days**, then travel by standard carrier. You'll get tracking by email as soon as it ships.

## Returns & exchanges

Because each shirt is made to order, we handle returns like this:

- **Our mistake — free fix.** Wrong item, a print flaw, or a defect? We reprint or replace it free. Send a photo to hello@flockthreads.com within 30 days.
- **Sizing exchange.** Ordered the wrong size? We'll help you exchange it — check the size chart on each product page first to get it right the first time.

## Questions

Email **hello@flockthreads.com** and a real person will get back to you.
